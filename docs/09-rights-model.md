# Bhu-Drishti 3D — Rights, Restrictions & Encumbrance Model

## 1. Overview
Bhu-Drishti 3D extends the ISO 19152 (Land Administration Domain Model - LADM) standard by linking legal rights ($RRR$ — Rights, Restrictions, Responsibilities) directly to 3D volumetric spatial units rather than flat 2D polygons.

---

## 2. Supported Rights Categories
1. **`OWNERSHIP`**: Fee-simple or leasehold title registered to natural persons, corporate entities, or cooperative housing societies.
2. **`MORTGAGE`**: Financial lien registered by authorized financial institutions (e.g., State Bank of India).
3. **`LEASE`**: Term-based tenancy agreements bound to vertical units.
4. **`EASEMENT`**: Servitudes granted across adjacent or common spatial units (e.g., shared corridors, staircases, fire egress corridors, utility duct rights-of-way).
5. **`RESTRICTION`**: Statutory or environmental constraints (e.g., coastal regulation zone limits, airport funnel height caps).

---

## 3. Flat 201 Hero Case Study
In Building B-17, **Flat 201** (`12345678901234/UB17-L02-201-X`) demonstrates multi-layered encumbrances:
- **Title Ownership (Green):** Karan Malhotra & Priya Malhotra (100% share).
- **Financial Lien (Red):** State Bank of India (Airoli Branch) — Registered Mortgage of ₹85,00,000.
- **Access Easement (Blue):** Building B-17 CHS Common Area — Egress easement for shared fire stairwell.

When switching the 3D Viewer to **Rights Mode**, Flat 201 turns **RED**, allowing banks, buyers, and town planners to visually detect active encumbrances instantly.
